## The Situation

GrowthSpace Agency had been in business for 4 years. They had great reviews, strong word-of-mouth, and a solid service offering. But online? They were invisible.

For their 12 core service keywords — the exact phrases their ideal clients were searching — they didn't rank in the top 50. Competitors were capturing leads that rightfully should have been theirs.

**They had been burned by two previous SEO "agencies"** that took retainer fees for 6 months each and produced nothing measurable.

---

## My Approach

I was brought in to scope the campaign, select a delivery partner, and oversee execution with a single KPI: **page 1 rankings for the 12 target keywords within 120 days.**

### Audit First

Before any action, I commissioned a full technical SEO audit. The findings were damning:

- **Page speed score: 31/100** — the site was loading in 7.2 seconds
- **No schema markup** on any service pages
- **Duplicate meta descriptions** across 40+ pages
- **Zero internal linking strategy** — every page was an island
- **Backlink profile: 12 referring domains**, mostly irrelevant directories

These were quick wins hiding in plain sight.

### The 90-Day Roadmap

**Month 1 — Technical foundation**
- Fixed page speed to 87/100 (core web vitals green across all)
- Implemented LocalBusiness and Service schema
- Rewrote all meta titles and descriptions with target keywords
- Built internal linking map with 3-click depth rule

**Month 2 — Content**
- Wrote 8 long-form service pages (1,800–2,500 words each)
- Created a city/neighbourhood landing page cluster targeting local intent
- Launched a blog with 4 posts targeting question-based queries ("how much does X cost in [city]")

**Month 3 — Authority building**
- Secured 18 relevant backlinks through outreach and partnerships
- 3 guest posts on industry publications (DA 45+)
- Fixed 7 broken citation inconsistencies across Google Business, Yelp, and directories
