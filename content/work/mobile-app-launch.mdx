## The Brief

LaunchPad Ventures had an idea for a B2B productivity app targeting operations managers in the logistics sector. They had technical co-founders, a seed round, and 18 months of runway.

What they didn't have: a clear go-to-market strategy, a development partner, or anyone who could translate business goals into a product roadmap that developers could actually build.

**I was brought in as the Business Development lead** to close the development partnership, manage the business-product relationship, and own the commercial launch strategy.

---

## What I Did

### Partner Selection

Evaluated 6 development studios across 3 markets. Selection criteria went beyond price:

- **Portfolio in B2B tools** — not just consumer apps
- **Experience with enterprise authentication** (SSO, permissions layers)
- **Post-launch support model** — not just a build-and-bill shop
- **iOS + Android parity** — required from day one

Selected and negotiated a fixed-scope contract with a 10% retention clause tied to App Store approval and 30-day stability.

### Roadmap Alignment

Ran a 3-day product workshop with the founding team and the development lead to align on:

- Core user journeys (prioritized to MVP — we cut 60% of the initial feature wishlist)
- Definition of "done" for each sprint
- Beta testing criteria and feedback loop structure

The hardest conversation I ran: convincing the founders that a simpler app launched 3 months earlier was worth more than a perfect app launched 3 months late.

### Beta Program

Recruited 40 beta users from the target segment through direct LinkedIn outreach. Ran structured feedback sessions every 2 weeks during the 3-month development period.

**Key insight from beta:** The original onboarding flow required 11 steps before the user saw value. We restructured it to show value in 3 steps. This change alone improved 7-day retention from 22% to 61% in internal testing.

### Launch Strategy

- Coordinated App Store submission (both iOS and Android simultaneously)
- Wrote App Store listing copy optimized for search visibility
- Built a pre-launch waitlist of 2,300 sign-ups through targeted LinkedIn and newsletter outreach
- Coordinated a Product Hunt launch (ranked #4 Product of the Day)

Three things:

3. **Pre-launch community building** — the 2,300 waitlist meant Day 1 had real organic momentum, not a cold launch
