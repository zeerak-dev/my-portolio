## The Problem

RetailTech Co. had a functional website — but functional wasn't enough. Despite spending $8,000/month on paid ads, their conversion rate sat at 0.8%. Visitors were landing, looking, and leaving.

The site was built five years ago, hadn't been updated since, and had no clear value proposition above the fold. The checkout flow had seven steps. Product images were low resolution. On mobile, the experience was unusable.

**The ask:** Fix it. Fast. And make the numbers move.

---

## My Role

I was brought in as the Business Development lead to scope the project, align stakeholders, manage the agency relationship, and ensure delivery matched the business objective — not just the brief.

### Phase 1: Discovery & Scoping (Weeks 1–2)

Before writing a single brief, I ran a structured discovery process:

- Reviewed 6 months of Google Analytics data to find the highest drop-off pages
- Conducted 5 user interviews with existing customers
- Ran a competitive audit against the top 3 SERP competitors
- Mapped the existing checkout flow and documented every friction point

The finding: **73% of cart abandonments happened between cart and payment**. The problem wasn't traffic — it was the experience.

### Phase 2: Vendor Selection & Brief (Weeks 3–4)

I sourced and evaluated 4 development agencies, shortlisted 2, and negotiated a contract that tied 20% of the fee to a post-launch conversion rate milestone.

The brief I wrote had one clear KPI: **get the conversion rate from 0.8% to at least 2% within 90 days of launch.**

### Phase 3: Execution & QA (Weeks 5–16)

- Managed weekly stand-ups between client and agency
- Reviewed every design decision against conversion research
- Personally QA'd mobile experience across 6 device types before sign-off
- Pushed back on 2 design choices that prioritized aesthetics over usability

### Phase 4: Launch & Optimisation

Post-launch, I set up a 30-day monitoring cadence and worked with the internal team to run A/B tests on the hero banner and product page layout.

## The Results

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conversion Rate | 0.8% | 3.4% | +325% |
| Avg. Order Value | $47 | $68 | +45% |
| Monthly Revenue | $22K | $91K | +312% |
| Cart Abandonment | 89% | 61% | -28pts |

**The site paid for itself within 47 days of launch.**

---

## What I Learned

The biggest lever wasn't the redesign — it was the **checkout restructure**. We reduced 7 steps to 3, added guest checkout, and made the shipping cost visible earlier. That single change drove 60% of the conversion improvement.
